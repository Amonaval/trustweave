# Next Mission — TrustWeave Discovery & Product Exploration

## Why this is next
MPF / Family Community and Residential are now feature-rich enough for controlled real-world pilots. The largest remaining adoption risk is not missing domain functionality; it is that a Chairman, President, Director or member cannot yet see the power of TrustWeave quickly enough before signing in.

## Mission thesis
**Reveal value before asking for commitment.**

The anonymous front door must become a product-exploration experience rather than an authentication-first screen. Login remains obvious and available, but it should not dominate the first impression.

## Three-depth experience
### 10 seconds
Explain what TrustWeave is and why a Society Chairman / Community President / Family organizer should care.

### 2 minutes
Role-specific journeys, flagship capability stories and direct Playground entry for Family Community and Residential.

### Go deep
A searchable Product / Doc Center exposing public-safe product guides, feature handbook, UX handbook, capability catalog, evolution journey and mission evidence through progressive disclosure.

## Primary audiences
- Housing Society Chairman / Secretary / committee
- Community / Cultural Association President / Director / committee
- Family organizer
- ordinary member, including teenagers and older non-technical users

## Required work
1. Anonymous landing IA redesign.
2. Chairman and President value journeys.
3. Capability explorer organized by goals/outcomes, not internal module names.
4. Playground as a prominent no-commitment proof path.
5. Product Knowledge / Doc Center linked to curated public-safe artifacts.
6. Keep founder-private, security-sensitive and unreleased-strategy documents physically excluded from the public document catalog.
7. Guided depth: simple summary → feature detail → workflows/examples → technical/product evidence.
8. Strong responsive/mobile behavior and accessibility for 15–50+ age range.
9. Conversion path: Explore → Try Playground → Understand setup/import → Sign in / Create.
10. Instrument which journeys/features visitors explore before signup, without collecting private network content.

## Guardrails
- Do not expose every archived/internal document publicly.
- Do not disclose founder strategy, patent candidates, trust-routing internals, anti-abuse logic or unreleased roadmap.
- Preserve existing authentication, My Networks, Playground and vertical functionality.
- No new vertical feature expansion during this mission unless real pilot feedback reveals a blocker.

## Exit condition
A Chairman or President who has never seen TrustWeave should be able to land on the site, understand the product, explore their own use case, try a realistic Playground and know why/how to onboard — without the founder continuously explaining the UI.
