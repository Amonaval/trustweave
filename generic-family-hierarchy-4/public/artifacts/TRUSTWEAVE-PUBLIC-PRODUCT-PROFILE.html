TrustWeave · Public Product Profile

Private networks that actually work.

TrustWeave is a governed Network OS for families, cultural/community associations, residential communities and other real-world networks. It combines people, relationships, history, operations, notifications and private media without collapsing every context into one public social graph.

The problem

Important communities are usually fragmented across spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, directories, forms, photo folders and isolated admin tools.

TrustWeave’s answer

Give each real-world group its own private, structured network: people, membership, relationships, history, responsibilities, activities and operations—while preserving network boundaries and role-based permissions.

Three flagship experiences

Family

Remember people, relationships and history

Profiles, relationships, generations, family tree, path/kinship context, memories, special days, claiming, contributions and family administration.

Community / Cultural Association

Run a member-family community

Households, representatives, membership/renewal, events, birthdays, committee, announcements, professional discovery, history and private photos. MPF Pune East is the flagship proving configuration.

Residential / Housing Society

Operate a society in one place

Flats, residents, notices, complaints, amenities, maintenance, governance, visitors/security, assets and compliance, with a Chairman-first operational Home.

Engagement without becoming a noisy social feed

Notifications

Persistent network-aware inbox, priorities, unread counts and deep links to the exact relevant item.

Web Push

Opt-in browser push can bring a user back even when the app is closed; the in-app notification remains authoritative.

Mentions & responsibilities

Network-scoped roles such as President, Chairman, Treasurer, Facilities or Complaint Resolver route attention to the right person.

Private media

Photos are resized, re-encoded to WebP, thumbnailed and served with private signed access. Complaint media remains stricter than ordinary event/community media.

Trust model

Network isolation

Each network remains independently governed. Hiding a vertical from new creation never removes an existing legitimate membership.

Authorization-safe deep links

A notification can point to a destination but cannot grant access. Membership and domain permissions are rechecked.

Governed network-of-networks

Federation, bridges, discovery and introductions are designed around explicit trust and consent rather than global graph exposure.
