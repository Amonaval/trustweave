# TrustWeave — One-Person + AI Company Operating Model

**Status:** CANONICAL COMPANY-OPERATING DIRECTION  
**Effective:** 2026-09-23  
**Principle:** One Founder + governed AI should be able to operate almost all digital company work until evidence proves humans add more leverage.

This is not “many AI personas pretending to be employees.” It is one company system with explicit accountabilities, tools, evidence, budgets and Founder gates.

## North-star operating model

> **Founder supplies purpose, taste, relationships and consequential judgment. AI turns that into continuous company execution.**

AI should increasingly handle:
- research and opportunity discovery;
- portfolio analysis and mission selection proposals;
- product management and UX criticism;
- architecture and implementation;
- QA, security, accessibility, performance and reliability review;
- documentation and knowledge maintenance;
- release preparation, observability and incident analysis;
- customer-feedback synthesis;
- demo/support/documentation preparation;
- market/competitor research;
- sales/pilot preparation and CRM hygiene;
- content/website/help-center preparation;
- finance/admin/legal/compliance research and drafting;
- operating metrics and Founder briefings.

The Founder should not be the message bus between these functions.

## Founder-only / high-consequence gates

Founder approval remains mandatory for:
- durable company/product direction changes;
- naming/brand decisions and public promises with material reputation impact;
- pricing/business-model commitments;
- contracts, company/legal filings and legally binding communications;
- payments/spend or any potentially billable resource not already pre-approved;
- hiring, investment/fundraising, equity and major partnerships;
- production release while the current release gate remains in force;
- destructive customer/production data actions;
- privacy/security risk acceptance or permission widening;
- irreversible federation/public-data decisions.

AI may prepare options, evidence, drafts and recommended decisions for these gates.

## Company control plane

The Founder should see one compact decision surface, not dozens of internal threads.

Each item should contain:
- decision needed;
- why now;
- recommended choice;
- strongest alternative;
- evidence;
- downside/risk;
- expected impact;
- cost/spend implication;
- deadline only when real;
- what AI will do automatically after approval.

Everything else should proceed within delegated policy.

## Operating loops

### Continuous loop

**Observe → understand → prioritize → execute → verify → publish/release when authorized → measure → learn → compact knowledge.**

### Daily / event-driven

AI should:
- ingest new product/user/runtime evidence;
- classify blockers vs opportunities vs noise;
- continue the single active priority;
- repair bounded failures without Founder relay;
- surface only D3/high-impact choices.

### Weekly Founder review

Keep it compact:
- real-user/adoption movement;
- highest-value user pain learned;
- current mission outcome/status;
- product/reliability/security exceptions;
- commercial/pilot pipeline;
- external spend/cost risk;
- one recommended next move;
- decisions requiring Founder judgment.

### Monthly / stage-gate review

Ask whether evidence changes:
- product stage;
- active proving vertical;
- commercial timing;
- AI investment;
- architecture/scale needs;
- company/legal/paid-infrastructure readiness.

Do not advance merely because a calendar month passed.

## Business-function autonomy

### Product / UX
AI can discover friction, propose/execute bounded improvements and validate journeys. Real-user evidence outranks AI taste.

### Engineering / architecture
AI should own normal implementation end-to-end inside constitutions, branch boundaries and mission scope.

### QA / security / performance
AI selects risk-based gates, classifies failures, repairs within budget and escalates only true high-impact uncertainty.

### Customer success / support
AI may draft help, diagnose reproducible issues, synthesize feedback and propose product changes. External messaging should use approved tone/claims and never invent guarantees.

### Sales / partnerships
AI can research prospects, prepare personalized demos/material, maintain pipeline context and draft outreach. Founder remains the relationship/commitment owner until explicitly delegated.

### Marketing / content
AI can create and maintain public-safe product explanations from canonical truth. It must not leak confidential strategy or claim unverified capabilities.

### Finance / legal / administration
AI can organize evidence, draft documents and flag obligations. It must not move money, accept contracts or make legal claims autonomously.

## When to add humans

Do not hire because “a startup should have a team.”

Add humans when evidence shows sustained leverage from:
- relationship-heavy sales/partnerships;
- regulated/legal/accounting responsibility;
- physical/on-site operations;
- specialized domain expertise;
- support volume that benefits from human empathy/judgment;
- engineering/product scale where additional human taste or ownership has higher ROI than more automation.

Even after hiring, preserve the AI-first operating system so human headcount does not become the coordination architecture.

## Company-memory rule

The company should remember decisions, not conversations.

Durable memory should capture:
- accepted direction;
- why a decision was made;
- evidence;
- outcome after use;
- reusable rule/lesson;
- supersession.

Do not preserve every transient discussion as always-on context.

## Success test

One-Person + AI Company is working when:
- Founder interventions per ordinary mission approach zero;
- raw error/log relay approaches zero;
- repeated network onboarding requires less engineering;
- product decisions remain anchored to real-user evidence;
- security/reliability do not decay with speed;
- high-value work reaches users faster;
- documentation/context shrinks while alignment improves;
- the Founder spends more time on customers, judgment and life than on routine digital operations.
