What changed as the product matured

1. Product before platform theater

Family forced privacy, emotional usability and real relationship semantics before generic architecture was extracted.

2. Reuse without semantic flattening

Shared identity, membership, media, activity and admin capabilities are reused; a spouse is not relabeled as a colleague and a flat is not treated as a family member.

3. One account, many private contexts

My Networks and federation work preserved independent governance instead of creating one universal readable graph.

4. Capability accumulation required UX subtraction

NX/showcase missions progressively hid advanced complexity, restored broken entry flows and built role/context-specific flagship homes.

5. Engagement now becomes the product loop

E1–E5 added the infrastructure needed for notifications, responsibility routing and photos to bring users back for meaningful actions.
