## Outcome

Make TrustWeave's architecture, mission lifecycle, evidence model and execution loop sufficiently explicit that a bounded engineering mission can be planned, implemented, verified, independently reviewed, documented and packaged with minimal founder participation.

## Boundaries

This mission does not introduce microservices, event sourcing, Kubernetes, an LLM framework, a new database, or broad in-product AI behavior. It reuses Git/CI, existing QA, source gates and the current repository structure.

## Authority

The binding product and architecture constitutions remain at repository root. Machine-readable projections live under `governance/`. Mission-local decisions and evidence live in this folder or `release-evidence/m3-b/` and cannot override constitutions.
