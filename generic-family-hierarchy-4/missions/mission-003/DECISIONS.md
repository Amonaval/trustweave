# Mission 003 Decisions

1. Use six accountable engineering roles rather than many always-on personas.
2. Activate Security, Data/Migration, UX/Accessibility and Performance as risk-triggered specialist lenses.
3. Use Git/branch/worktree/PR/checks as the durable execution control plane; no multi-agent framework is required for B1-B6.
4. Treat deterministic scripts as primary truth for facts they can prove; use model reasoning for architecture, ambiguity, review and failure diagnosis.
5. Maintain two evidence classes: source/static and runtime. They may never be conflated.
6. Preserve M3-A's progressive selector convergence as the first R1 agent-governed experiment.
7. B1/B2 contracts are binding for B3-B6; constitution changes remain founder-gated.
