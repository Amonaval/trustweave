# M3-C — Autonomous Company Runtime / Founder Spectator Mode

## Intent

TrustWeave should become an AI-operated company, not merely an AI-assisted codebase. The founder remains owner of vision, values, product identity and irreversible boundaries; AI becomes the default operating layer for strategy, product, architecture, engineering, QA, criticism, release, knowledge and continuous improvement.

The target experience is **Founder Spectator Mode**: the founder can watch decisions, debates, risks, missions and outcomes, but routine progress does not depend on the founder relaying errors, remembering context, sequencing tasks, requesting documentation updates or repeatedly asking what comes next.

## What extraordinary means

The system must eventually be able to take a broad intent such as:

> Improve the Housing Society pilot experience and make it significantly more compelling without weakening Family Community or platform architecture.

and autonomously perform:

1. evidence discovery and user-friction analysis;
2. executive-role debate (CEO / CTO-Architect / User Advocate / Critic / Chief of Staff);
3. opportunity ranking and mission decomposition;
4. architecture and scope decisions;
5. implementation in bounded branches/worktrees;
6. targeted and regression verification;
7. failure classification and bounded self-repair;
8. independent adversarial review;
9. documentation/evidence synchronization;
10. preview/release/rollback planning;
11. post-release observation and learning;
12. selection of the next highest-value mission.

## Founder involvement

Default founder involvement should approach zero for reversible routine work. Founder approval remains required for D3 decisions defined in `governance/autonomy-policy.json` until explicitly delegated.

## Success condition

M3-C succeeds when one meaningful TrustWeave product/architecture mission can move from founder intent to a release-ready candidate with **zero manual error relay**, **independent review**, **durable state**, and **no more than one non-mandatory founder intervention**.
