# Housing Society Pricing Experiment Playbook

Pricing remains a hypothesis until pilot evidence exists.

## Models available for testing
- per unit / month
- flat annual society fee
- base platform + paid modules
- setup / migration fee
- undecided / discovery

## Record
For every conversation capture the model, amount, currency, response (`untested`, `rejected`, `neutral`, `interested`, `accepted`) and the reason/context.

## Do not infer
An interested committee is not the same as a paid renewal. A founder discount is not market price. A migration-heavy society may value setup differently from recurring software.

## Commercial proof
The strongest signal is a second society that can be onboarded repeatably and expresses credible willingness to renew/pay after actual weekly use.
