# Housing Society Pilot — Resident Guide

This pilot is designed to replace scattered society information with one governed resident experience while the committee tests what genuinely saves time.

## What residents can use
- My Flat and resident directory
- society notices
- complaints/service requests and status tracking
- amenities/bookings
- maintenance dues/receipts visibility
- committee meetings/resolutions where published
- visitor pre-approval, move/renovation requests and emergency contacts
- community events and memories

## Claiming your profile
Use only the invitation sent for your resident identity. The verified email must match the invitation. Claiming a resident profile does not allow a resident to change official ownership or tenancy records.

## During the pilot
Use the product for normal society work where enabled. If a task still has to happen only on WhatsApp, a spreadsheet or a manual register, tell the committee: HS-6 intentionally records those gaps.

## Privacy
Resident access remains society-scoped. Official property, finance, security and governance actions keep their existing authorization boundaries.
