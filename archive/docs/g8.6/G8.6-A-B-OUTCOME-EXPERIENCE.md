# G8.6-A + G8.6-B — Outcome-Driven Vertical Experience

## Mission
Move the non-Family products from generic network surfaces toward buyer-relevant product jobs without copying Family vocabulary.

## G8.6-A — Orientation & Entity Action
Delivered:
- fixed shared `.modal-backdrop` so productized entity/editor dialogs render as true fixed overlays;
- added reusable `NetworkStructureView` with vertical projection switching and entity drill-in;
- added Structure Map / Guided Drill-down modes to Organization, Business Trust and Franchise;
- added Alumni living-structure augmentation while retaining protected Alumni runtime semantics;
- expanded entity detail into a 360° action surface: View in Network, explain connection path, update connection, ask network, edit where permitted;
- relationship explorer can open focused from an entity and uses vertical-specific question language.

## G8.6-B — Living Knowledge & Help Network
Delivered:
- reusable `NetworkKnowledgeHelpHub`;
- Organization: **Wins & Lessons**, **Communities of Practice**, expertise/ownership/problem requests;
- Business Trust: **Trust Evidence & Success Stories**, **Business Circles**, verification/supplier/warm-path requests;
- Franchise: **Operations Playbook**, **Operator Networks**, peer-location/problem-solving requests;
- Alumni: **Alumni Journeys & Give Back**, chapters/cohorts/interest circles and mentoring/introduction prompts;
- generic contribution is reframed as Help the Network with domain-native prompts while retaining governed admin review;
- activity/group foundations remain shared and tenant-scoped.

## Deliberate boundaries
- Family kinship, ancestry, generations, deceased/remembrance semantics remain Family-owned.
- G8.6-A/B do not claim paid intelligence yet; G9 remains responsible for evidence-backed recommendation/intelligence engines.
- full Platform Owner Launch Control parity and domain-native Home/Guide/return loop remain G8.6-C.

## Exit result
The newer verticals now answer more useful questions than “what nodes exist?” Users can understand structure, inspect an entity in context, trace/update relationships, browse reusable knowledge, enter focused communities and initiate a help request.
