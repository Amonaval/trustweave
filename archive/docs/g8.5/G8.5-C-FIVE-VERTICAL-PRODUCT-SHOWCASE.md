# G8.5-C — Five-Vertical Product Showcase & Certification

## Mission
Prove that the Generic Network OS now presents five credible, explorable products rather than one mature Family product plus shallow templates.

## Showcase standard
A Playground must contain enough entities, relationships, geography, groups and network-life activity to exercise the major applicable capabilities. It must also explain useful journeys so novice users can discover why the network matters.

## Certified showcase scale
- Family Network: 60 demo members with relationships, memories, life events, geography and community flows.
- Alumni Network: 36 alumni across 7 batches, 6 programs, cities and companies; 6 chapters/circles and 10 activity items.
- Organizational Intelligence: 36 people, ~50 typed matrix relationships, 6 guilds/communities and 10 activity items.
- Business Trust Network: 36 businesses, ~50 recommendation/work/verification/supply relationships, 6 partner circles and 10 activity items.
- Franchise Network: 36 locations across 9 cities, ~50 support/operating/management/ownership relationships, 6 operating groups and 10 activity items.

## UX closure
- Added G8.5-C What's New cards to Alumni and productized vertical Playgrounds.
- Added three guided showcase journeys to Organization, Business Trust and Franchise Home.
- Added Guide showcase-proof sections with explicit dataset/capability breadth.
- Retained shared Light/Dark/Aurora theme infrastructure and added theme-safe showcase cards.
- Added responsive layouts for showcase journeys, metrics, hero actions and What's New actions.
- Launch Control remains permission-aware: platform/founder release ownership is not transferred to network admins.

## Architectural rule preserved
Showcase richness is configuration/data and shared capability composition. No Family-only semantic concepts were pushed into other verticals, and no vertical imports another vertical implementation.

## Exit criterion
G9 may begin only after the historical source chain plus `validate:g8-5c` are green and the five Playgrounds can demonstrate useful network behavior at meaningful sample scale.
