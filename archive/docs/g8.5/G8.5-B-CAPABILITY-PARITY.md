# G8.5-B — Generic Capability Parity

## Mission
Raise Organization, Business Trust and Franchise from productized-template depth toward mature Network OS products by reusing generic capability engines rather than copying Family semantics.

## B1 — Discovery & Relationships
- Added shared `NetworkGeography` with interactive Leaflet map, location coverage summary and entity drill-down.
- Added shared `NetworkRelationshipExplorer` with typed relationship filtering, shortest known connection path, relationship metrics and admin relationship editing.
- Added shared `NetworkEntityDetail` so Directory, Explorer, Map and Relationships can open a consistent detail view.
- Productized Playground data expanded from 5 to 12 primary entities per business vertical with coordinates and denser relationship graphs.

## B2 — Living Network & Participation
- Fixed shared Activity Hub so Events and Announcements are discoverable to ordinary members and Playground users; role rules still govern creation.
- Existing shared Groups, RSVP, History/Story, Milestone and Announcement engines remain the source of truth.
- Governed Contribution now captures a contribution intent: Correction, Missing information, Relationship, Verification or Opportunity.
- Productized verticals continue to use neutral network language; Family remembrance/deceased semantics remain Family-only.

## B3 — Lifecycle, Governance & Help
- Productized identity claiming, join-code membership, entity import, member/admin governance and governed contributions remain wired through the productized network persistence adapter.
- Network Admin now receives a read-only Launch Control status summary. It does not gain platform-owner rollout mutation rights.
- Guide now explains the actually available Network OS capabilities and their rollout state, with direct navigation into each surface.
- No alternate Family/Alumni administration path was introduced.

## Architecture rule applied
When a capability is semantically reusable, the implementation lives in `components/shared`, `capabilities`, or `core` and verticals provide labels/configuration. Domain semantics remain vertical-owned.

## Explicitly deferred to G8.5-C / later
- 30–60 entity showcase datasets per business vertical (G8.5-C; B raises them to 12 for meaningful interaction).
- Full outbound notification delivery/digest scheduling. Current vertical definitions preserve the concept but B does not claim a new delivery backend.
- AI/network intelligence remains G9 and is still gated on G8.5-C certification.
