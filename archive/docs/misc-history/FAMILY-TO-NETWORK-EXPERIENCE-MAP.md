# Family-to-Network Experience Translation Map

**Purpose:** audit the mature Family product feature-by-feature and translate the *user job* into each other vertical where it creates real value. This is not permission to copy Family semantics.

Legend:
- **BUILD** — should become a first-class experience in the target vertical.
- **ADAPT** — shared job applies, but terminology/workflow differs materially.
- **KEEP** — already reasonably present; deepen rather than recreate.
- **DOMAIN** — Family-specific; do not genericize.
- **N/A** — no meaningful analogue.

## M. Family capabilities that should remain Family-only

Do not genericize simply for parity:

- kinship vocabulary and inference;
- ancestor/descendant lineage semantics;
- spouse/parent/child rules;
- deceased/in-memoriam behavior;
- marriage-family semantics;
- family-specific birthday/anniversary rituals;
- family privacy conventions that do not match enterprise/business roles;
- family-specific emotional copy.

The reusable product lessons are **orientation, explanation, contextual history, trusted contribution, community activation, guided discovery and meaningful return loops**.

## Family as a Trusted Network Seed Model

Family is no longer only a vertical proving ground. It may be the **trusted-network seed model** for community ecosystems.

Potential governance hierarchy: **Individual → Family Network → Community/Association Umbrella → selective trusted connections to other networks**. This is a governance hierarchy, not automatic data sharing.

Family-derived capabilities especially relevant to network effects include claiming/identity, invitations, contributions, trusted introductions, relationship paths, community groups, events, memories/history as trust/context, profiles, distributed intake, help-network-grow loops and multi-family/community linking.

Future cross-network use cases should reuse these primitives with explicit consent and domain-specific terminology.
