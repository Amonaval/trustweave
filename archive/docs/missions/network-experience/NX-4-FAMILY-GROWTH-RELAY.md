# NX-4 — Family Growth Relay

## Outcome
Make family growth feel like a shared family habit, not an organizer/admin burden.

## Product loop
Notice one useful gap → help directly or pass the question → governed profile/contribution flow → invite another relative when needed → family knowledge compounds.

## Implemented
- `FamilyGrowthRelay` prioritizes small deterministic preservation gaps from existing Family profiles.
- Relationship intelligence explains the target relative in the signed-in member context when possible.
- “Ask someone” creates a privacy-safe share/copy prompt; it does not expose contact data or make edits.
- Existing contribution suggestions remain available below the new human-friendly relay.
- Admins can move directly from the relay to the existing secure invitation workflow.
- Playground remains read-only.

## Guardrails
No new database schema, no auto-generated facts, no bypass of profile/claiming/review permissions, no cross-network exposure, no engagement gamification.

## Success signal
More useful family information is completed by ordinary relatives with less organizer effort.
