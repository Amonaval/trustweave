# NX-9 — My Networks Contextual Guidance & WOW Refinement

## Outcome
NX-8 solved the information architecture. NX-9 improves comprehension and delight without reopening that structure.

## Implemented
- contextual info affordance on the active My Networks area;
- contextual info affordance on the active advanced tool;
- learning-card explainers on the overview;
- tailored modal content rather than one repeated FAQ template;
- concept mental models, usage moments, privacy/guardrail callouts and recommended next moves;
- additional active-state, focus, transition, depth and modal polish;
- mobile bottom-sheet-style help presentation;
- public/evolution living artifacts updated.

## UX constitution
`Everyday network → advanced journey → one focused tool → explanation only when requested.`

The goal is expert capability without expert-only navigation.

## Non-goals
- no feature/permission/Launch Control changes;
- no NF/M6/M7 internal workflow rewrite;
- no new My Networks structural navigation layer;
- no always-visible help text that reintroduces scrolling and cognitive overload.
