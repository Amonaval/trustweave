# G9.1 Organization Pilot — Buyer Test Playbook

## Objective
Prove whether a real organization will exchange data, attention and budget for **expertise + ownership + dependency-risk intelligence**.

## Pilot hypothesis
A technical organization with 50–500 people has enough hidden knowledge/ownership/dependency complexity that an explainable network can reduce expert-finding time, coordination friction and key-person risk.

## Minimum pilot data
- people;
- teams/departments;
- role/title;
- skills/expertise tags;
- products/services/platforms;
- project/ownership affiliations;
- explicit reports-to / works-with / owns / supports / depends-on relationships;
- optional captured incidents/lessons/decisions.

Do not require email/chat surveillance for the first pilot.

## Five demo questions that must feel valuable
1. Who understands **X** best and why?
2. Who owns **Y**, and who is the nearest backup?
3. What depends on **Y**?
4. What becomes risky if **Person Z** leaves?
5. Who should I involve for **change/incident/project X**?

## Success metrics
- median time to find a credible expert/owner before vs. with the product;
- number of unknown/missing owners resolved;
- critical capabilities with >1 verified knowledgeable person;
- useful introductions/actions initiated from intelligence;
- repeat weekly usage by pilot users;
- buyer willingness to continue/pay.

## Interview questions
Do not ask “Do you like it?” Ask:
- What did you do before this?
- How often does this problem happen?
- What does a bad answer cost?
- Which answer here would change a real decision?
- What data would you refuse to upload?
- Who approves this purchase?
- What budget/tool would it replace or complement?
- Would you run this for 30 days with your team?
- Would you pay for that pilot?

## Stop criteria
Pause or narrow if, after several serious buyer conversations:
- nobody will provide real data;
- the questions are interesting but not recurring;
- users already get satisfactory answers from existing systems;
- privacy/onboarding cost exceeds perceived value;
- there is no budget owner.

The goal is commercial truth, not continuation for its own sake.
