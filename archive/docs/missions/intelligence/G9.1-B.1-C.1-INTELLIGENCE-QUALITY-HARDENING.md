# G9.1-B.1 + C.1 Intelligence Quality Hardening

## Why this mission exists
Northstar manual validation exposed two concrete quality problems: explicit entities in ownership/dependency questions could be replaced by people with strong evidence/affiliation matches, and seven knowledge documents produced too few review candidates.

## C.1 query hardening
- Exact/longest entity label explicitly present in the question wins before fuzzy search.
- Ownership always traverses `? --owns--> target`.
- `What depends on X?` traverses incoming `depends_on` edges.
- `What does X depend on?` traverses outgoing `depends_on` edges.
- Combined upstream + downstream dependency questions return both directions.
- Expertise uses verified skill signals plus evidence-backed skill assertions for ranking.
- Default answer UI is concise; supporting evidence and technical graph reasoning are collapsed with progressive disclosure.

## B.1 extraction hardening
- Two focused extraction passes per evidence item: ownership/dependency and expertise/decisions.
- Larger source window (6500 chars) and larger response budget.
- Explicit guard against treating planned future ownership as current ownership.
- Candidate de-duplication.
- Coverage diagnostics expose raw candidates, accepted candidates, filtered candidates and skip reasons.
