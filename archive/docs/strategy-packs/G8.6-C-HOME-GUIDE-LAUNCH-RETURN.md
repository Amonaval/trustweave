# G8.6-C — Home, Guide, Launch & Return Loop

## Mission
Close the final pre-G9 product-experience gap by making every active vertical answer three questions immediately: what matters now, what can I do next, and why should I return?

## Delivered
- Outcome-driven Home command center for Organization, Business Trust, Franchise and Alumni.
- Domain-specific buying-value language instead of generic graph statistics.
- Return Loop with current activity, knowledge/evidence, community and next-best-action prompts.
- Goal-based mature Guide focused on outcomes rather than feature enumeration.
- Contextual What's New available in Guide, not only Playground banners.
- Full platform-owner Launch Control reachable from Alumni and all productized business verticals.
- Launch Control opens on the current vertical while preserving owner/admin permission separation.
- Responsive/theme-safe UI for Light, Dark and Aurora.

## Product thesis reinforced
The graph is infrastructure. The product value is the loop:

Find → Understand → Connect → Act → Capture → Improve.

G9 may now build intelligence on top of a product surface that already exposes useful actions and return reasons.
