1. Run `npm ci`, `npm run build`, then `npm run validate:g8-6c`.
2. Open Family and verify its existing Home/Guide/Launch Control behavior is unchanged.
3. Open Alumni, Organization, Business Trust and Franchise Playground/Home.
4. Verify outcome Home cards render correctly in Light, Dark and Aurora.
5. Verify Home cards navigate to Connections, Community, Contribute and Guide.
6. Verify mobile layout stacks command-center, signal and return-loop cards cleanly.
7. Sign in as ordinary member/admin and confirm Launch Control is not exposed.
8. Sign in as platform owner and verify Launch Control appears from Alumni and each business vertical.
9. Verify Launch Control opens preselected to the active vertical and rollout changes still follow platform-owner permissions.
10. Open Guide and verify goal-based journeys plus contextual What's New.
11. Verify existing Explorer, Directory, Community, Places, Connections, Contribute and Admin flows still work.
