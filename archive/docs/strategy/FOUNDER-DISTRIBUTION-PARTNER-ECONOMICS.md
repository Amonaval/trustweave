# Founder Distribution Partner Economics

**Status:** High-priority founder distribution doctrine — 2026-08-29  
**Goal:** Maximize verified network activation and retained usage with the least founder selling effort, least cash paid upfront and least equity dilution.

## 1. Core principle — pay for compounding value, not introductions
A partner is economically valuable only when the relationship produces a durable asset: a launched governed network, activated members, an umbrella that brings child networks, retained engagement, paid revenue, or adjacent-network referrals. Raw leads, imported names and one-time registrations should not receive the same economics as activation.

**North-star unit:** `Partner Cost / Retained Activated Network`, supported by cost per retained activated member and number of downstream networks created by the partner.

## 2. Default incentive ladder
Use the cheapest reversible incentive that can unlock the required distribution leverage. Move upward only after evidence.

| Layer | Partner type | Default economics | Founder cost/risk | Best use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Advocate / member referrer | recognition, product benefits, referral credit, small success reward | Very low | Organic introductions and community champions |
| 1 | Activation agent | milestone bounty paid only after verified launch/activation/retention | Low and variable | Individuals who can onboard local families, chapters, associations or businesses |
| 2 | Growth partner | tiered activation bounty + time-bounded revenue share on revenue actually collected from sourced accounts | Variable, self-funding | Repeat operators who launch multiple networks |
| 3 | Supernode / umbrella partner | network-level activation economics + recurring share tied to retained child networks/revenue | High leverage, still mostly variable | Associations/federations that can activate many independently governed networks |
| 4 | Territory / operating partner | performance-based economics, optional limited territory protection after milestones | Moderate | Strong regional operators who do onboarding/support repeatedly |
| 5 | Strategic partner | milestone-vesting small equity/equity-like participation plus commercial economics | Highest and irreversible | Rare partners providing exceptional distribution, regulatory access, brand, capital or institutional leverage |

## 3. Recommended default — Agent + Activation + Retention
The broad partner layer should resemble a high-quality agency network, but compensation must reward outcomes rather than policies sold or names collected.

Example measurement ladder:
1. qualified network introduced;
2. network created and governed owner verified;
3. minimum useful dataset/onboarding completed;
4. target active-member threshold reached;
5. 30/60/90-day retention or meaningful recurring usage reached;
6. adjacent network or umbrella referral activated.

Pay progressively across those milestones rather than paying everything at signup.

## 4. Large-community multiplier
To encourage partners to pursue distribution supernodes instead of only easy small groups, compensation may increase when **verified retained activation** crosses network/community thresholds.

A safe structure is:
- base activation reward for a small viable network;
- network-size multiplier after a retained-member threshold;
- additional multi-network multiplier when one organizer launches several independent networks;
- supernode bonus when an umbrella creates a repeatable child-network funnel;
- retention multiplier only after the agreed verification window.

Do not pay linearly for imported member count. Otherwise partners are incentivized to upload low-quality names.

## 5. Revenue share — preferred over large upfront cash
Revenue share aligns cost with realized revenue and can be attractive to professional operators.

Default doctrine:
- share **cash actually collected**, not theoretical contract value;
- attribute the sourced network/umbrella clearly;
- use a defined earning window unless a strategic contract justifies longer participation;
- step down the rate as TrustWeave itself takes over ongoing acquisition/support;
- allow enhanced share for downstream child networks only when the partner materially activated them;
- preserve founder ability to change future pricing without retroactively breaking earned economics.

## 6. Exceptional equity — scarce and milestone-vesting
Equity is the most expensive incentive because it is permanent and compounds if the company succeeds. It should never be the default referral mechanism.

A strategic partner should pass most of these gates:
- can unlock distribution that would otherwise take the founder years;
- has durable institutional authority or reach, not merely contacts;
- contributes repeatedly after introductions;
- creates an asset that survives the individual relationship;
- is difficult to replace;
- has already produced evidence before meaningful ownership vests.

Prefer **earn-in / milestone vesting** over unconditional equity. Commercial rewards should remain the first layer; equity is an additional rare accelerator.

## 7. Hybrid models worth testing
### A. Agent-to-Operator ladder
Referral → first activation bounty → higher tier after N retained networks → recurring share after commercial threshold → territory/operator status.

### B. Supernode accelerator
Umbrella receives a modest activation pool for verified child networks plus revenue participation on monetized child networks. Bigger upside comes from more retained governed networks, not access to member data.

### C. Earned strategic stake
Small ownership/equity-like participation vests only after agreed distribution milestones such as retained networks, annual revenue or strategically important umbrella activation.

### D. Deferred acquisition bounty
Instead of large cash payments now, partners accumulate earned credits payable from later revenue from the accounts they activated. Useful during founder-funded early scale, subject to clear contractual caps and accounting/legal review.

### E. Reseller / managed-launch partner
Partner sells onboarding/setup/managed operations while TrustWeave retains the software relationship and platform governance. Can scale founder capacity without creating employees.

### F. Institutional champion program
Non-commercial volunteers/champions can receive recognition, premium product benefits, community status or event/support benefits where cash incentives would distort trust.

## 8. Anti-gaming and quality gates
Partner economics must be tied to verifiable events:
- distinct governed networks, not duplicate shells;
- legitimate owners/admins;
- claimed/active participants rather than imported names alone;
- retention/meaningful activity thresholds;
- no self-referral loops or duplicate attribution;
- fraud/reversal clawback;
- cap total acquisition cost as a percentage of expected contribution margin/LTV.

## 9. Partner attribution ledger — future product capability
Eventually record:
`partner → introduced umbrella/network → activated child networks → retained members → paid revenue → adjacent referrals → earned economics`.

This should become a founder dashboard and later a governed partner portal. It is commercially sensitive and should not be mixed with public TrustWeave member reputation.

## 10. Decision rule for any new incentive
Before offering a richer deal, answer:
1. What measurable bottleneck does this partner remove?
2. What non-copyable asset will exist after the incentive is paid?
3. Can we pay after evidence instead of before?
4. Can revenue share replace cash?
5. Can commercial economics replace equity?
6. If equity is necessary, can it vest only on hard outcomes?
7. Does the deal improve `retained activated networks / founder hour`?

## 11. Founder optimization target
The desired system is not a large payroll or expensive field-sales force. It is a distributed network of economically motivated operators where **most acquisition cost is variable and success-funded**.

> **Use cash for verified activation, revenue share for durable commercialization, and equity only for exceptional asymmetry.**
