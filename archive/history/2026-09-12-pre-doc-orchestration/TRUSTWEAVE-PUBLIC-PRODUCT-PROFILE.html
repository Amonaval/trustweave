Public product profile · rebuilt August 2026

A governed Network OS for people, private networks and the ecosystems they choose to join.

TrustWeave began as a personal Family hierarchy app and evolved into a reusable product: people can participate in many private networks, peer networks can establish governed trust, and networks can independently affiliate with domain-specific communities or federations—without turning everything into one public social graph.

App → product → platform-backed product

It is no longer only an app.

The original Family experience remains a real application and proving ground. The larger system now has repeatable roles, multiple verticals, shared identity and privacy contracts, Launch Control, governed network trust, runtime boundaries, evidence loops and a new federation model. That makes “product” a correct description. Traction will determine product-market fit and whether it becomes a product company.

Corrected structural model

Two many-to-many dimensions. No forced universal hierarchy.

The person dimension and the federation dimension are independent. A person may belong to many networks; each of those networks independently affiliates with the umbrellas appropriate to its own domain.

Defensibility strategy

Protect the mechanism. Quietly compound the network.

TrustWeave should not rely on feature lead alone. The founder strategy is protect → seed → prove → compound → amplify: selectively protect technically meaningful inventions, quietly build dense institutional networks, and let verified provenance plus successful outcomes become the hardest asset to copy.

2026-08-29 · Network Federation

Federation as a distribution multiplier

TrustWeave is evolving federation so trusted umbrella organizations can help independent networks onboard and participate at scale while each child network retains its own governance and private graph. Federation participation does not imply person-level access.

2026-08-29 · Network Federation

Network Passport: outward identity without exposing the private graph

Each governed network can now maintain a deliberate network-level Passport with a summary, broad geography, capabilities, purpose declarations and explicit visibility. A public Passport is a network identity page—not a member directory. Federation-only visibility remains non-public, and declared purpose scopes never opt people into applications.

2026-08-29 · Network Federation

Governed Network ↔ Umbrella affiliation

A network can now request institutional affiliation with a community, association, federation or other umbrella using its Federation/Public Network Passport. Umbrella administrators explicitly approve, decline, suspend or revoke the relationship. Approval verifies the network-level relationship only: it never enrolls people, exposes member directories or inherits private graph topology.

Platform direction

From proven verticals to composable network types

Family, Alumni, Professional, Organization, Business Trust and Franchise are reference products. The long-term platform direction is a governed Network Type Studio where terminology, entity/relationship models, modules, workflows and application scopes can be composed into many domain-specific network products without rewriting the platform.

Federation operations

Govern networks without absorbing their private graphs

An umbrella or association can operate a directory of approved participating networks, understand federation readiness and see only the outward Network Passport information each source network currently permits. The umbrella does not automatically inherit the people, contacts or relationships inside those networks.

Federated network discovery

Participating networks can now discover other outward-facing networks through governed umbrella affiliations while preserving source-network privacy. Discovery explains the institutional path and remains network-level; individual participation requires separate purpose-specific consent.

2026-08-29 · Purpose-scoped federation

People participate by purpose, not by global exposure

A participating network may declare that it supports jobs, expertise, business, mentoring or another application purpose, but individual members are never enrolled automatically. A person must explicitly publish a selective profile for that exact purpose and can withdraw it independently. This creates useful federation applications without turning TrustWeave into a global people directory.

NF-6 Trusted Request Routing

TrustWeave can now turn a purpose-scoped need into explainable candidate routes through approved federation paths and explicit participant opt-ins. Routing remains separate from contact or introduction: a suggestion does not reveal private contact details or imply endorsement.

NF-7 — Consent before contact

A shortlisted trusted route can now become a governed introduction. The recipient sees the request and institutional provenance first; deliberately supplied response channels unlock only after explicit acceptance.

2026-08-29 · NF-8

Trust reaches an outcome without becoming a public score.

Outcome + Trust Receipt

After a governed introduction is accepted, each participant can privately record what happened. A Trust Receipt preserves the request, institutional route and consent timeline that made the connection possible. Outcomes stay scoped to that connection and are not exposed as universal person ratings.

My Networks Guided Control Center

TrustWeave now keeps everyday network use simple even when advanced trust, federation and outcome capabilities are enabled. Users start with their actual networks, choose advanced journeys in plain language, and open one focused tool at a time. Federation and trusted-request workflows are taught progressively rather than presented as a wall of expert controls.

Guidance appears when complexity appears

Advanced TrustWeave workflows now include contextual explainers instead of forcing users to learn platform terminology upfront. A user can ask what an area or step means and see a concise mental model, when it is useful, important privacy boundaries and a natural next action. The result is a progressively revealed product: simple for everyday participation, deeply capable when advanced trust, federation or outcome workflows are needed.

29 Aug 2026 · Real-user productization

Community / Association

TrustWeave now includes an active Community / Association vertical for household- or member-based organizations. It combines registered families/households, representatives, annual membership status, events and RSVP, memories, announcements, committees and governed participation while preserving network privacy.

Family-grade Community / Association

Community / Association now combines household membership with full person profiles, family relationships, birthdays, events, memories, annual membership, scoped family co-administration and community history. Its visual identity uses a refined indigo, gold and saffron palette while preserving privacy and generic Network OS architecture.

Family Community / Cultural Association

TrustWeave now includes a distinct family-centric association composition for cultural/community societies where the paid membership unit is a family but every person remains first-class. It reuses Family-grade profiles, relationships and contribution patterns with annual membership, events, community history, controlled leadership roles, finance visibility and safe network lifecycle. The generic Community / Association template remains separate.

New vertical · HS-0

Residential Community / Housing Society

TrustWeave now has a first-class, unit-centered housing-society foundation: Society → Building/Tower → Wing → Floor → Unit/Flat → Household → People. The Unit/Flat is the primary operating object, while ownership, tenancy, occupancy and household membership remain explicit relationships instead of inferred profile facts.

HS-0 reuses the platform's hierarchy, directory, community, Playground, guides and Launch Control engines. Complaints, maintenance billing, amenities, governance, security and compliance stay intentionally staged for later HS missions after their domain permissions and temporal history are implemented.
