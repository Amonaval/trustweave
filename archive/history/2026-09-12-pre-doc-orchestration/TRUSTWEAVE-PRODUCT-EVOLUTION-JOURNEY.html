Trajectory-changing decisions

The moments that changed what the product was.

Family semantics were not universal

G0/G1 rejected cosmetic relabeling. Family kinship, Alumni membership and Business relationships became explicit vertical semantics over shared primitives.

Deployment was not release

B0-B created progressive Launch Control; LC-1 later corrected M6/M7 when advanced features bypassed that invariant.

Network effect needed privacy

M6 deliberately chose anonymous discovery + consented reveal rather than a universal cross-network directory.

More features stopped being the default

M7 ended with feedback, runtime certification and an explicit product-decision gate so future work follows evidence.

The second dimension appeared

Once Network↔Network trust matured, the next unmet model was Network↔Umbrella affiliation—independent, many-to-many and domain-specific.

“App” became “product”

Multiple verticals, reusable runtime/governance, release controls, evidence loops and a coherent value proposition moved the boundary beyond one personal application.

2026-08-29 · NF-1

Network Passport made federation outward-facing without making private graphs public.

Passport before affiliation

Problem: federation needed a stable identity for each participating network, but reading child-network member tables would violate the privacy model. Shift: NF-1 introduced one governed network-level Passport with private, federation-only or public visibility, plus capabilities and inert purpose declarations. Unlock: NF-2 can now attach explicit affiliation provenance to a controlled outward identity instead of merging graphs.

2026-08-29 · Network Federation

NF-0A — Federation as Distribution Supernode

The federation track began with a distribution-focused foundation: aggregate-only scoring helps identify umbrella anchors that can activate many governed child networks. The implementation explicitly preserves the privacy rule that network affiliation never grants implicit access to members or private graph topology. Real Network Passport and governed affiliation persistence follow in NF-1 and NF-2.

2026-08-29 · NF-2

Institutional provenance became a governed graph instead of an implied hierarchy.

Affiliation is not access

Problem: a network needed to prove membership in a wider community/federation without making the umbrella a parent owner of its people or graph. Shift: NF-2 introduced first-class umbrella anchors, Passport-gated requests, explicit umbrella review, and suspend/revoke lifecycle. Unlock: approved affiliations can now become distribution and provenance signals for NF-3+ while private source networks remain independently governed.

2026-08-29 · NF-3

Umbrella Network Runtime

Federation became operational without becoming centralized: approved child networks are the participants, Passport metadata is the outward boundary, and aggregate readiness/capability views avoid querying private child-network graphs. This is the first usable network-of-networks operating surface.

NF-4 — Federated Directory & Discovery

TrustWeave moved federation from an operating view to governed discovery. Users can find directory-enabled Network Passports through approved umbrella paths and see why each network is reachable, without centralizing child-network members or inferring person consent.

NF-5 — Purpose-Scoped Applications

Federation gained its missing person-consent layer. A network may declare a purpose, but each person independently opts into one network + umbrella + purpose context through a selective outward snapshot. This enables future community applications while preserving default private-network isolation.

NF-6 Trusted Request Routing

TrustWeave can now turn a purpose-scoped need into explainable candidate routes through approved federation paths and explicit participant opt-ins. Routing remains separate from contact or introduction: a suggestion does not reveal private contact details or imply endorsement.

NF-7 — Consent before contact

A shortlisted trusted route can now become a governed introduction. The recipient sees the request and institutional provenance first; deliberately supplied response channels unlock only after explicit acceptance.

NF-8 — Outcome + Trust Receipt

The first federation trust loop now closes with private bilateral outcome evidence and a durable provenance snapshot. TrustWeave can preserve why a connection occurred and what each participant says happened without creating a universal public reputation score.

NX-8 — From organized workspace to guided control center

Runtime feedback on NX-7 showed that structural grouping alone was not enough: users could still miss that the journey labels were navigation and could still feel overwhelmed inside each journey. NX-8 introduces unmistakable outcome-based navigation, everyday/admin cues, quick-start intents and a second progressive-disclosure layer that renders one capability at a time.

NX-9 — Advanced capability becomes self-explaining

TrustWeave now teaches advanced areas at the moment a user needs them. Small contextual guide controls explain the mental model, the right moment to use a capability, the privacy boundary and the next sensible action without permanently expanding the page. This preserves progressive disclosure while reducing the expertise required to navigate federation, trust and outcome workflows.

29 Aug 2026 · Real-user productization

MPF-A0 · Community / Association

A real family-centered association use case exposed a gap between Family and Alumni. Rather than force-fit either vertical, TrustWeave promoted its Association template proof into a reusable active vertical. The first composition centers households, representatives, annual membership and community life, while formal elections remain a separately governed future capability.

MPF-A1 — Family-Grade Association Experience

Real MPF-style requirements showed that a member association is not a flat directory. The vertical evolved toward Family-grade people and household structure while retaining Association membership, events, committees and broader-network readiness. This created the second deeply evolved TrustWeave vertical from a real deployment need rather than speculative roadmap breadth.

FCA-0 — Family Community Association

Problem: the generic Association proof was too flat for real family-cultural communities such as MPF. Reasoning: preserve generic Association while composing mature Family primitives with association operations. Change: introduced family-association with full people/family directory, temporal annual membership and leadership history, finance ledger, familiar home, annual operations, engagement and safe lifecycle. Unlock: a credible 10-family real-world pilot without exposing Generic Network OS complexity.

New vertical · HS-0

Residential Community / Housing Society

TrustWeave now has a first-class, unit-centered housing-society foundation: Society → Building/Tower → Wing → Floor → Unit/Flat → Household → People. The Unit/Flat is the primary operating object, while ownership, tenancy, occupancy and household membership remain explicit relationships instead of inferred profile facts.

HS-0 reuses the platform's hierarchy, directory, community, Playground, guides and Launch Control engines. Complaints, maintenance billing, amenities, governance, security and compliance stay intentionally staged for later HS missions after their domain permissions and temporal history are implemented.

HS-1 · Property, Household & Resident Core

Housing Society now preserves owner/tenant/occupancy history, supports resident My Flat claiming, vehicles/parking and mapped 20–50 unit onboarding. Runtime verification remains required before HS-2.

HS-2 — Housing Society Daily Operations

Housing Society now moves beyond structure into daily utility: notices, resident complaints with committee SLA handling, vendors/contracts, amenities/bookings and Coming Up, while the mature shared events/RSVP/memory timeline remains reused. HS-3 billing is intentionally separate.

HS-3 — Maintenance, Dues & Finance

Housing Society now represents a complete flat-level maintenance cycle: configurable charge heads, billing cycles, immutable bill lines, dues, payments/receipts, adjustments, arrears, funds and budget-vs-actual visibility. Payment gateway and accounting integration remain deferred until pilot evidence.

HS-4 — Housing Society Governance

Housing Society now preserves committee terms, meetings, minutes, action items and controlled member resolution history. Election-grade secret ballots remain a separate future capability.

HS-5 — Housing Society Security & Compliance

Housing Society now includes scoped visitor/staff operations, move and renovation approvals, asset service history, compliance due dates and emergency contacts without granting operators full society-admin rights.

Housing Society HS-6 — Founder Pilot & Commercialization

The housing vertical now closes the source roadmap with a measurable four-stage pilot: internal demo, 20–50 real units, full society, then a second-society repeatability proof. Readiness derives from actual onboarding, resident activation, notice reach, complaint resolution, maintenance, governance and operational evidence; admins can record time saved, remaining offline work, renewal/willingness-to-pay and pricing experiments without hard-coding commercial assumptions.
