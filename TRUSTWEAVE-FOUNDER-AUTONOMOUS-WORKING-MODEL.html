Credentials

Your Supabase secrets do not need to be shared

The complete connected certification runs on your machine against a dedicated staging Supabase. Credentials remain in ignored .env.qa. The generated qa-results/ contains the certification summary, bug report, remediation plan, coverage matrix, findings, browser traces/screenshots/videos and database/RPC/RLS evidence. Share that result bundle—not the environment file—for autonomous remediation.

The resilient full crawl now runs one isolated role × vertical shard at a time across all nine verticals, checkpoints every result, retries one clean context, never clicks destructive controls, and reports flaky harness behavior separately from reproducible failures.

Founder control remains mandatory for:
  • destructive or irreversible production action;
  • material privacy/security risk acceptance;
  • recurring spend or external commitments;
  • inaccessible infrastructure and credentials;
  • fundamental product or constitutional changes.
